Independent, but not alone.

Most of my work has been freelance. That usually raises one question, so here is the direct answer to it.

I work inside a team, not beside one

Part-time at Brandsite since April 2026, designing e-commerce and corporate sites with a product manager and iterating on their feedback. On Parfee, Fiducia, Xdose and Smartinterior I worked alongside PMs — and on Fiducia directly with the development team.

I write the brief when there isn't one

Clients arrive without specs. I run competitor and audience research, and build CJMs, user stories, JTBD and information architecture before any interface exists.

I hand off systems, not screenshots

Tokens, variables, states and variants, structured for engineering. The TextYourTenant team said the system was easy for their engineers to implement — that is the outcome I design handoff for.

I design the whole state, not the happy path

Empty, loading, partial, failed, and every combination a role can actually encounter. On multi-role products this is most of the work.
